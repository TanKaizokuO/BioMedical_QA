Guidelines — read once, then refer back

Judge attribution, not truth. Label each claim by what the highlighted span says. A claim can be false but supported (the passage says it), or true but unsupported (correct medicine, absent from the span). Use no outside knowledge.

SUPPORTEDThe span asserts the claim.
PARTIALThe span asserts part of the claim, or asserts it more weakly or more narrowly than stated.
NOT_SUPPORTEDThe span does not address the claim.
CONTRADICTEDThe span asserts the opposite of the claim.

All spans together is a separate judgement: read every highlighted span for that claim as one piece of evidence and label the claim against the whole. Two citations can be jointly necessary — a dose from one span and an outcome from another is correctly cited with both.

Well-formed claim asks only whether the sentence is a self-contained statement you could check at all — no dangling "it"/"this study", not a fragment, not a question. It is not about support. Answer the support questions either way.

Work in the order given and finish each question before moving on. Your progress is saved in this browser, and — when the header says backed up — copied to the lab machine as well. Download the JSONL at the end of every session and send it in: that file is your own copy. Use one browser on one machine. If you must change machine, or the browser loses your work, press Restore… and pick a copy; nothing is ever merged for you.
